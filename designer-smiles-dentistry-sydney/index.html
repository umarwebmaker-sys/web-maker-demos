Services

Cosmetic and restorative dentistry

Whether you want to restore function or refine how your smile looks, our team supports you every step of the way.

Porcelain Veneers

Thin porcelain shells that refine the shape and shade of your teeth for a natural-looking finish.

Smile Makeover

A plan that combines treatments, such as veneers, crowns or a gum lift, to transform your whole smile.

Dental Implants

Options for a single missing tooth, multiple missing teeth or a full-arch All On 6.

Clear Aligners & Orthodontics

Straighten teeth and close gaps or spaces with discreet clear aligners or orthodontic care.

Stained Teeth

Treatment for tooth discolouration so your smile looks brighter and more even.

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Prosthodontist-led rebuilding of worn teeth, with crowns, so you can eat and smile comfortably again.

We also offer periodontics, TMD/TMJ care, sleep apnoea treatment, sleep dentistry, pregnancy and oral care, and telehealth consultations.
