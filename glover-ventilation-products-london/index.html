What we supply

The core ranges, explained in plain words.

Spiral Ductwork

Rigid round duct for main air runs in supply and extract systems.

Flexible Duct

Bendable duct for awkward routes and final connections to grilles and units.

Plastic Duct

A lightweight duct option for ventilation runs.

Dampers

Control or shut off airflow through the duct system.

Grilles

Neat room outlets that let air in or out where it's needed.

Access Doors

Panels that give easy access to ductwork for inspection and cleaning.

Also available: channel and support systems, flange systems, tapes and sealants, fixings, fasteners and flashing, and insulation material.
